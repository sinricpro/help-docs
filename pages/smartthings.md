Sinric Pro works with **Samsung SmartThings**. After you link your account, your Sinric Pro devices appear in the SmartThings app and can be used in SmartThings scenes and routines.

### Link Sinric Pro in SmartThings

First, download the SmartThings app for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.smartthings.android&hl=en) or [iOS](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/smartthings-mobile/id590800740?mt=8), create an account and log in.

* Open the SmartThings app and tap the **+** Icon.
* Select **Device**.
* Select **Sinric Pro** under **By Brand**.
* Link your account.

Now you can turn your devices on or off from the SmartThings dashboard.

### Troubleshooting

**The device does not appear in SmartThings**

- Check that the device exists in the [Sinric Pro Portal](https://portal.sinric.pro) and is online.
- Check that SmartThings is linked to the same Sinric Pro account that owns the device.
