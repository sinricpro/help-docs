The Device Templates in SinricPro feature a simple push button in the SinricPro mobile app that triggers actions on your device when pressed.
 

#### Supports

- [ ] Alexa
- [ ] Google Home
- [ ] SmartThings
- [x] SinricPro Portal
- [x] SinricPro App

Not Supported
