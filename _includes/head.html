Cloudflare Pages redirects /page.html to /page, and Polyglot builds every page under / and under each language prefix (/pt/, /de/, /it/, /es/). Each page gets one clean canonical URL: English content at /pages/x, translated content at //pages/x.. A translation is the file x..md with "lang: " in its front matter. static_href stops Polyglot from adding a language prefix to these links. Which languages a page exists in comes from site.translations (see _plugins/translations.rb).
